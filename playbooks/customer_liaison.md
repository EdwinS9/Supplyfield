# Customer liaison playbook (version 0)

## Principles

- Lead with what it costs if nothing is done: copies at risk, the hour a part runs out.
- Show the evidence: which file, which place, what it says, in the original words.
- One request per owner: list the records to fix, most costly first, at most ten in one request.
- Never blame a person. Describe the record, not who edited it.
- A request that nobody can act on is worse than no request. If you cannot say what to do and by when, do not send it.
- Offer the proposed value so the owner only has to confirm or correct it.

## When to act alone

- A defect has an owner role and the evidence is clear: send the request.
- A question for one of our own roles: show it as a notice with the full text.

## When to escalate

- The defect has no owner role: it is not yours. Leave it.
- The owner is a supplier: that goes through the supply liaison.
- The evidence is weak or both sources look equally reliable: say so in the request and ask which is right.

## Checks

- Does the title make sense to a busy person in five seconds?
- Is the evidence named with file and place?
- Is there one clear ask, and a deadline that is real?
- Has the same fix already been requested?

## Message style

- Plain words, short sentences, no system jargon.
- Title: the cost and the record. Detail: two or three sentences. Ask: one sentence starting with a verb.
